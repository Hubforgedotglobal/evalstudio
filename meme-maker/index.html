Open MEL Meme Maker

Free to use. No account required. Personalised in your browser.

How it works

  1. Choose one of 12 original editable compositions.
  2. Personalise the joke, colours and format with a live preview.
  3. Download a square or landscape PNG, or an SVG. Explicitly saved designs stay in My Collection on your device.

Good to know

Custom text is your responsibility. Keep jokes friendly, respectful and workplace-safe. This is a template editor, not an AI image or joke generator.

Personalised words and explicitly saved designs remain on your device. Unsaved edits reset when you reload. Keep downloaded files separately if you need a lasting copy.
