Workshop Playbox

Make evaluation workshops more participatory. Start with an activity, adapt it to your session and prepare useful materials for people to work with.

Open Workshop Playbox

Free to use. No account required. Personalised in your browser.

How it works

  1. Choose evaluation bingo, assumption challenges, reflection prompts, voting cards, Theory of Change cards or retrospective sheets.
  2. Edit the content for your group. Use facilitator guidance and choose low-ink settings when useful.
  3. Download the current page as A4 PNG or SVG, or print the activity using your browser. Save as PDF through the print dialog.

Good to know

A4 PNG pages are 2480 × 3508 pixels. For printing, choose A4, actual size and no margins, and disable browser headers and footers.

Personalised words and explicitly saved designs remain on your device. Unsaved edits reset when you reload. Keep downloaded files separately if you need a lasting copy.
