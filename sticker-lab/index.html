Small designs for the people asking big questions. Make stickers about research, evidence, fieldwork, responsible AI and learning together.

Open Sticker Lab

Free to use. No account required. Personalised in your browser.

How it works

  1. Choose one of 32 original designs in six collections.
  2. Personalise the words, colours, typography and output format with a live preview.
  3. Download a PNG or SVG. Save favourites and designs in My Collection on your device.

Good to know

Use designs in presentations or share them as images. A downloaded image is not an automatically installed WhatsApp sticker pack.

Personalised words and explicitly saved designs remain on your device. Unsaved edits reset when you reload. Keep downloaded files separately if you need a lasting copy.
