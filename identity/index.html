Identity

Express who you are, what you contribute and what matters to you in monitoring, evaluation and learning. These cards are self-expression, not verified credentials.

Open Identity

Free to use. No account required. Personalised in your browser.

How it works

  1. Choose a style and a curated prompt, then add your own name, role and statement.
  2. Optionally add a photo, adjust the composition and colours, and preview your card.
  3. Download a feed, Story or square PNG. Save a card on this device to reopen and remake it. Keeping a photo in a saved card is optional.

Good to know

Photos are processed locally, not uploaded. Image sharing depends on browser support; downloading is available as an alternative. Reusable template links contain style and prompt identifiers, never personal text or portraits.

Personalised words and explicitly saved designs remain on your device. Unsaved edits reset when you reload. Keep downloaded files separately if you need a lasting copy.
